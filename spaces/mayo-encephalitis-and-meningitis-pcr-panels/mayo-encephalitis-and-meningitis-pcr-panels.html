Mayo Clinic Laboratories · Test catalog research · Oct 30 – Nov 2, 2026

There is no Mayo blood version of the meningitis / encephalitis PCR panel.

Mayo's only syndromic meningitis/encephalitis PCR panel is CSFME — spinal fluid only. For blood, Mayo offers two tick-borne panels and a set of single-organism PCRs you order one at a time. And the catalog is the same worldwide — what changes is the site, the state, and the exposure, not the country.

The three answers, up front

Everything below is the evidence for these three points, with exact test IDs, specimen types, and organism lists from Mayo's catalog.

1 · Blood panel?No multiplex blood panel reproduces the CSF panel's 14 organisms. Blood PCR is TIKLB + BRBST (tick-borne) plus single-organism tests.
2 · Different by country?Not supported. One Mayo catalog, shipped to Rochester from 80–130+ countries. Different lists abroad are usually local, non-Mayo panels.
3 · Is “ARBO” a panel?No Mayo ID “ARBO” exists. The closest are ARBOP / ABOPC — arbovirus antibody panels (IFA), not PCR.

Meningitis workup, per Mayo's algorithm: for acute illness (<8 days) it starts with lumbar puncture + CSFME if on-site CSF testing is available, plus blood cultures (culture, not PCR), CSF cell count / protein / glucose, Gram stain and bacterial culture. Mayo says it is “not usually recommended” to also order CMVPV, LENT, HSVC or VZVPV when CSFME is ordered, and local public-health requirements may require additional testing on positives — retain an aliquot. The only true multi-organism blood panels

Both are tick-borne panels on whole blood. Neither is a meningitis panel, and neither reproduces the CSFME list.

TIKLB

Tick-Borne Panel, Molecular Detection, PCR, Blood

Whole blood EDTA · 1 mLNY: Yes
  • BABPB — Babesia spp. (B. microti, B. duncani, B. divergens, MO-1)
  • EPCRB — Ehrlichia / Anaplasma (E. chaffeensis, E. ewingii/canis, E. muris eauclairensis, A. phagocytophilum)
  • BMIPB — Borrelia miyamotoi (relapsing-fever B. hermsii / parkeri / turicatae may also be detected at a different Tm)
Does not detect B. burgdorferi or B. mayonii (Lyme). Mayo TIKLB page
BRBST

Tickborne Bacterial, PCR and Sequencing, Blood

Whole blood EDTA · 1 mL

Broad 16S rRNA PCR + next-generation sequencing identification of pathogenic tick-borne bacteria in blood, including organisms with a similar presentation — e.g. Coxiella burnetii (Q fever) and leptospirosis.

Not first-tier — only when routine testing is negative. Not a meningitis panel and not marketed for encephalitis. BRBST catalog mirror

Not a blood panel: BRBPS (Broad Range Bacterial PCR and Sequencing, Varies) covers sterile body fluids including CSF, and tissue — do not cite it as a blood panel.

No blood counterpart was found for these CSFME targets — do not assume one exists

The six CSFME bacteria as a blood multiplex · human parechovirus as a standalone blood/plasma PCR · VZV as a standalone blood PCR (VZVPV “Varies” lists CSF, body fluid, swab, respiratory and tissue — blood is not listed) · Cryptococcus by blood PCR (diagnosis on blood is antigen-based, not a Mayo blood PCR panel).

Broad tests that are not PCR panels — do not mislabel them

  • MSCSF — Bacteria, Virus, Fungus, and Parasite Metagenomic Sequencing, Spinal Fluid. CSF 2 mL, metagenomic NGS (not PCR), lumbar-puncture CSF only; shunt/ventricular fluid not acceptable. NY: Yes.
  • KPLA / KDPLA — Karius Spectrum, Plasma. Microbial cell-free DNA metagenomic sequencing, >1,000 organisms, performed by Karius Laboratory, ordered through Mayo. Not a Mayo PCR panel, not encephalitis/meningitis-specific.
  • FLARP — Free-Living Amebae, Molecular Detection, PCR, Varies. CSF 0.5 mL or tissue only — Acanthamoeba spp., Naegleria fowleri, Balamuthia mandrillaris. Not blood. NY: Yes.

By pathogen class — the short map

  • Viral, blood PCR: West Nile, Powassan, Heartland, HSV, enterovirus (plasma), HHV-6 (plasma), CMV (plasma), dengue / chikungunya / Zika (serum).
  • Bacterial: no blood meningitis multiplex. Blood is TIKLB + BRBST only. CSF/other: CSFME bacteria, BRBPS, MTBRP (M. tuberculosis complex), LYMPV (Lyme PCR, Varies).
  • Fungal: Cryptococcus in CSFME is the only fungal target in a syndromic panel. No blood fungal PCR panel found.
  • Parasitic: no blood parasitic encephalitis PCR panel found. CSF/tissue: FLARP. TOXB (Toxoplasma gondii PCR, Blood) appears on Mayo's microbiology request form as a single-organism test — its full catalog page was not verified here (see Open questions).

The “ARBO” question

You asked whether ARBO is a code for the meningitis panel. In Mayo's catalog, it is not — and it is not a PCR test at all.

No Mayo test ID “ARBO” exists.

“ARBO” / “ARBS” appear as local client / EHR codes that map to Mayo ARBOP — for example, Children's Minnesota lists lab code ARBS, “Referred to: Mayo Medical Laboratories (MML Test: ARBOP).” If a record says “ARBO panel,” check whether it means ARBOP, ABOPC, or a non-Mayo local arbovirus panel. It does not denote a Mayo PCR panel.

Other countries: one catalog, four things that actually vary

Mayo describes one open-access catalog, updated daily, whose tests are “available to patients around the world through Mayo Clinic Laboratories,” with MayoLINK ordering in eight languages and global logistics to extend specimen stability in transit. No country-specific Mayo encephalitis/meningitis PCR panel, renamed panel, or different organism list was found. Specialty Testing · Global Capabilities

A · Performing site, inside the US — Rochester vs Jacksonville

Jacksonville-only IDs, with Rochester equivalents for all other clients: LHSV / LHSVB (= HSVPV / HSVPB), LCMV (= CMVPV), LVZV (= VZVPV), LHSVZ (= HSVPV + VZVPV). One of these IDs on a report means the specimen went to Jacksonville, FL — not a different assay or country.

B · State — New York

The only state-level split found. CSFME = No and ABOPC = not available for NY-origin specimens, while ARBOP, WNVS, TIKLB, ENS2, MSCSF, POWVC, HRTVS, FLARP, and QHV6P = Yes. Each Mayo page carries its own “NY State Available” field.

C · Exposure geography — same IDs, different selection

Mayo's Mosquito-borne Disease algorithm (Mar 2024) branches on exposure: US exposure → ARBOP / ABOPC + individual virus panels; outside-US exposure → add chikungunya, dengue, Zika tests; any region → West Nile antibody + PCR. A non-US exposure gets a different selection, not a different panel. Algorithm (PDF)

D · Practical limits — stability and shipping

Specimen stability windows (e.g. CSFME refrigerated ≤7 days; serum arbovirus panels 14 days) and customs / import logistics are the binding international constraints found. Mayo addresses them with tailored packaging and carrier handling — not with a second catalog.

Where “different panels in different countries” really comes from: on-site panels abroad

Both are CSF-only. Neither is validated for blood. A clinician abroad reporting a “meningitis panel” with a different organism list is almost certainly reporting a local on-site panel like one of these — not a different Mayo panel.

7

Routed to CDC / public health instead of Mayo

No Mayo test ID was found for these — the route is your local public health department or the CDC.

  • Bourbon virus and Jamestown Canyon virus: Mayo's mosquito algorithm says, if suspected, “contact your local public health department or the CDC for testing options.” No Mayo PCR or antibody ID for either was found.
  • Heartland virus serology: Mayo's HRTVS definition states patients with symptoms >1 week may be PCR-negative and “may require serologic testing, which is available through the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention.” Mayo's Heartland PCR itself is in-house.
  • CSFME positives: Mayo warns there may be local public-health requirements requiring additional testing on positive specimens — retain an aliquot. This is a downstream obligation, not a Mayo-to-CDC referral of the panel itself.
  • Arbovirus confirmation: closely related flaviviruses cross-react serologically, and plaque-reduction neutralization may be needed — the kind of confirmatory work typically done by state public-health / CDC laboratories. No specific Mayo “send to CDC” panel ID was verified (open question).

Open questions — stated plainly

What the research could not verify. Nothing here was filled in by guessing.

  6. Pricing, turnaround times, and current NY status change frequently and were not the focus — re-check each Mayo page's “NY State Available” and “Report Available” fields at order time.
