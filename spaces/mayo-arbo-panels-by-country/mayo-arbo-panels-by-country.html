Mayo Clinic Laboratories · Arbovirus (ARBO) testing

One Mayo panel, many names — and different panels abroad

You asked whether other countries get a different Mayo ARBO panel than the one here, or whether it's the same test under different names. The answer is both happen — but not inside Mayo. Mayo itself runs one panel worldwide. The different panels belong to other countries' own laboratories.

Inside Mayo

Same test, different local codes

Mayo publishes one open-access test catalog, updated daily, performs the ARBO panel in Rochester, and states its protocols are applied uniformly regardless of geographic origin. Client labs abroad and in the US order that same Rochester test under their own local codes and names.

Outside Mayo

Different tests, similar name

Other countries' own labs run genuinely different “arbovirus panels” — different viruses, different methods. New Zealand's is only chikungunya, dengue and Zika by PCR. Australia's includes Ross River and Murray Valley. The name “ARBO” alone does not mean Mayo's 4-virus panel.

Direct answer: No country-specific Mayo ARBO panel was found. What changes by country or travel exposure is which separate tests get added — dengue, Zika, chikungunya, West Nile — not the ARBO panel itself. Those are individual Mayo test IDs, never bundled into ARBOP.

1Your US panel — confirmed exactly as you described it

Mayo's serum ARBO panel is 8 results: 4 viruses × IgG + IgM. There is no separate Jamestown Canyon analyte and no coronavirus analyte in it. The spinal-fluid version carries the same 4 viruses.

Same 4 viruses as the serum panel. One US-only wrinkle: ABOPC is not available for specimens originating in New York (ARBOP is). That is a state restriction, not a country-specific panel.

Mayo catalog — ABOPC (overview 83897)

About Jamestown Canyon: it is not a separate analyte in ARBOP or ABOPC. It appears in Mayo's mosquito-borne algorithm only as a virus to consider for CDC / public-health testing. La Crosse and Jamestown Canyon are both California-serogroup viruses and Mayo's page warns about cross-reactivity in general — but it does not say a positive California (La Crosse) result equals a Jamestown Canyon result. Don't treat it as one.

2Why there is no “Mayo panel for other countries”

Mayo Clinic Laboratories is one reference laboratory in Rochester serving, per its international shipping guide, more than 3,400 hospitals and laboratories in more than 60 countries. The differences abroad are shipping, customs and specimen-export rules — not a different test menu.

Don't confuse the names

Mayo Clinic Laboratories = the Rochester reference lab that performs ARBOP/ABOPC. That is the panel in question.

Mayo Clinic Healthcare, London = a clinic at 15 Portland Place, not a separate laboratory catalog. No London-specific ARBO panel was found; secondary UK sources describe London patients as having access to Mayo Clinic Laboratories testing.

Mayo representative offices (e.g. in the Caribbean) coordinate appointments and travel — they provide no clinical care or testing themselves. A local hospital abroad that lists a “Mayo test” is ordering the Rochester test as a send-out, not running a local Mayo variant.

One real exception, and it isn't a panel: some countries do not allow specimens to leave the country at all (China is the example given in a secondary magazine account), in which case Mayo partners locally. That changes where a specimen can go — not what ARBOP contains.

3What actually changes: test selection by exposure

Mayo does not fold travel-region viruses into ARBOP. Its mosquito-borne testing algorithm branches on where the exposure happened, and the extra viruses are ordered as separate tests alongside the panel.

ExposureMayo tests to considerTest IDs
Mosquito exposure in the US The ARBO panel itself, serum and/or spinal fluid — plus the same four viruses as single-virus panels. ARBOP / ABOPC; CAVP/CAVPC, EEEP/EEPC, STLP/STLPC, WEEP/WEEPC
Mosquito exposure outside the US Add separately: chikungunya antibody and PCR (serum and spinal fluid); dengue antibody, antibody/antigen panel, NS1 antigen and PCR; Zika IgM and PCR (serum and urine). CHIKV, CHIKS, CHIKC · DENGM, DENVP, DNSAG, DENGS, DENGC · VZIKM, VZIKS, VZIKU
Any region Add separately: West Nile virus antibody (serum and spinal fluid) and West Nile PCR (serum, blood, urine, spinal fluid). West Nile is never inside ARBOP. WNS, WNC · WNVS, WNVBL, WNVUR, WNCSF
Powassan / Heartland / Jamestown Canyon / Bourbon suspected The algorithm points to CDC / public-health testing — not a Mayo panel. Mayo does separately offer Powassan tests (IgM ELISA; PCR in blood, serum, urine), listed under tick-borne rather than mosquito-borne testing. POWV, POWVB, POWVS, POWVU

Two viruses with no Mayo test found

Yellow fever — no Mayo test ID found. Catalog searches returned no Mayo yellow-fever test; it appears on Mayo pages only as a cross-reactivity / specificity organism in other tests. Don't assume Mayo offers it.

Japanese encephalitis — no Mayo test ID found. Same pattern. Per CDC, JE diagnosis is serum/CSF IgM, and confirmatory neutralising-antibody testing is “only available at CDC and a few specialized reference laboratories,” with specimens routed via state/local health departments to CDC's Arbovirus Diagnostic Laboratory — a non-Mayo route. CDC: JE clinical diagnosis

Bottom line: no combined Mayo “international ARBO panel” bundling dengue + Zika + chikungunya + yellow fever + JE was found. Sources: Mayo mosquito-borne algorithm (PDF) · Mayo mosquito-borne disease menu · DENGM · CHIKS · VZIKS

4Same Mayo test, different name or code — yes, this is common

Every listing below is the same Rochester test: same 4 viruses, same IFA method, Mayo Test ID ARBOP/ABOPC. Only the ordering system's local code or name differs. These examples are US client labs — the same send-out pattern applies to any Mayo client abroad.

The 4-point check: is it really the Mayo test?

1

Performing lab

Mayo Clinic Laboratories in Rochester.

2

Mayo Test ID

ARBOP (serum) or ABOPC (spinal fluid).

3

Method

IFA — immunofluorescence assay.

4

Virus list

Exactly California (La Crosse), Eastern equine, St. Louis, Western equine.

If any of the four differs — PCR instead of IFA, dengue/Zika/Ross River in the list, a public-health lab as the performer — it is a different test, even if it is called an “arbovirus panel.”

PathWest — Western Australia

Non-Mayo

“Arbovirus Serology” — serum SEARB / CSF SEARC. A history-selected panel, IgM + IgG per selected virus: flaviviruses dengue, Zika, Japanese encephalitis, Murray Valley encephalitis, Kunjin/West Nile, yellow fever, plus alphaviruses chikungunya, Ross River, Barmah Forest. Ross River, Barmah Forest and Murray Valley are Australian-region viruses that are absent from Mayo ARBOP.

UKHSA Rare & Imported Pathogens Laboratory — United Kingdom

Non-Mayo

Geographic panels — no fixed “ARBO panel” name found. The panel is chosen by the laboratory based on region, symptoms, travel and duration. For acutely unwell returning travellers, Zika testing is done as part of a geographic PCR + serology panel without Zika having to be specifically requested. Covers dengue, chikungunya, Zika, West Nile and others as selected — not Mayo's fixed 4-virus IFA.

Non-Mayo · Public health

“Arbovirus – Serum (ARBO)” / “Arbovirus – CSF (ARBOCSF)” — the clearest warning case. Same “ARBO” stem as Mayo, different test: the panel “may change throughout the testing season” and may include West Nile, Powassan, Jamestown Canyon, EEE, WEE, California-group and St. Louis — broader than ARBOP, and it includes the Jamestown Canyon analyte Mayo's panel lacks.

6What could not be verified — open questions, stated plainly

  • No explicit Mayo denial exists in the sources found. Mayo does not publish a statement saying “there is no country-specific catalog.” The one-catalog conclusion rests on the single daily-updated catalog, the uniform-protocol statements, and the absence of any country-specific ARBO listing in searches. Confidence: high for ARBOP/ABOPC's composition; medium for the absence of a country-specific panel.
  • Yellow fever and Japanese encephalitis at Mayo: not found, not proven absent. No Mayo test ID for either was returned by catalog searches — they appear only as cross-reactivity/specificity organisms. Mayo's referred-tests list was not fully searchable, so a referral route via Mayo cannot be ruled out. No such Mayo ID is cited on this page.
  • No country-by-country Mayo price or availability table exists in the sources found. Fees are account-specific (“contact your U.S. or International Regional Manager”). Whether a specimen may legally leave a specific country must be checked with Mayo's global team and that country's export rules at order time.
  • Jamestown Canyon cross-reactivity is unverified. Whether a California-serogroup (La Crosse) IFA result in ARBOP would cross-react with Jamestown Canyon antigen is not stated on Mayo's ARBOP page, which discusses dengue and EEE/WEE cross-reactivity only. Do not infer a Jamestown Canyon result from ARBOP.
  • Live-page check failed during research. Two attempts to open Mayo's ARBOP page in a live browser returned an error with no page. Every Mayo fact here comes from catalog/index fetches (pages crawled 28–30 Oct 2026, plus the mosquito-borne algorithm PDF) and is consistent across Mayo and multiple client-lab relistings — but re-check the live catalog before ordering.
