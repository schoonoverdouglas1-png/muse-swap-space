Conjugate

Streptococcus pneumoniaePCV7 / PCV13 / PCV20 / PPSV23

Serotypes covered

PCV7: 4, 6B, 9V, 14, 18C, 19F, 23F · PCV13 adds 1, 3, 5, 6A, 7F, 19A · PCV20 adds 8, 10A, 11A, 12F, 15B, 22F, 33F · PPSV23: 23 valences

Carrier / Mechanism

CRM197 — capsular polysaccharide conjugated to toxoid, converting T-independent to T-dependent response. PPSV23 is pure unconjugated CPS.

Danish numerical serotyping standard, based on structural CPS variation.

Serogroups

A, C, W-135, Y (MenACWY) · B (MenB)

Carrier / Mechanism

CRM197, DT or TT for MenACWY. MenB uses OMV / recombinant proteins — fHbp, NadA, NHBA.

Antigen

Polyribosylribitol phosphate (PRP) capsule

Carrier / Mechanism

CRM197, TT, or OMPC — converts a weak pediatric T-independent response into T-cell dependent memory.

The classic proof that conjugation changes what a child's immune system can remember.

Target

Toxigenic strains producing diphtheria toxin

Mechanism

Formalin-inactivated diphtheria toxin — both a disease-preventing vaccine and a carrier platform for conjugates.

Fungal elements

Bacterial pathogens rely on diverse capsular polysaccharides. Major fungal pathogens use analogous cell-wall and capsular polymers to evade host immunity — the other half of the continuum.

Capsule

Cryptococcus neoformans / gattii

Primary virulence factor is the GXM capsule, alongside galactoxylomannan (GalXM). GXM is common to the whole neoformans/gattii complex; serotypes divide by mannose-backbone substitution. Experimental GXM–protein conjugates use CRM197 or TT.

Cell wall

Candida albicans / auris

Mannan and β-glucan complexes; outer fibrillar phosphopeptidomannan in albicans. C. auris is distinct — outer mannan enriched in β-1,2 linkages with unique M-α-1-phosphate side chains, recognized by macrophage mannose receptor and CR3. The PPM label was verified for albicans, not assumed for auris.

Biomarker

Aspergillus fumigatus

Galactomannan is both a key diagnostic biomarker and a cell-wall component — another high-molecular-weight polysaccharide posing clearance challenges similar to encapsulated bacteria. Biofilm matrix galactosaminogalactan is filed with the calcium-pathways note below.

Serotyping & serology — connecting illness

The through-line of this repository: a serotype is not just a label. It is the capsule, the antibody reaction, and the illness pattern, read together.

What serotyping gives you

A common language for variation — Danish pneumococcal numbers, meningococcal serogroups, GBS Ia–V — so the same organism can be tracked across vaccine, lab report, and patient.

What serology adds

The host side of the same picture: which antibodies are present, what they cross-react with, and where a grouping holds, breaks, or is still a gap. Shiro is the wildcard column in these tables — Ember's place for extra information and questions, labelled as her addition, shown not hidden.

Postulates — kept separate from the tables

Douglas's rule: postulate separate from table. A table must be useful on its own. A postulate is where a new connection is stated plainly, before it is tested.

Postulate · Nov 5

Fungal analogues to bacterial CPS

Fungal capsular and cell-wall polysaccharides function as analogues to bacterial CPS — and can therefore be mapped, and potentially conjugated, on the same architecture. Source synthesis via Gemini share, tested item-by-item in this repository.

How this repository works

01
Save first.

Work is pushed to save as a research repository, bypassing peer-review gates for saving. Public is fine — it is for research purposes only. Formal publishing is a separate gate.

02
Old with new.

Corrections sit beside the original, never silently replacing it. The PCV carrier correction and the closed gattii gap above are the working examples.

03
Logic first, then fact-check, then verdict.

Source facts, external research, hypotheses, and unknowns are kept distinct, with labels — documented, hypothesis, gap, partial, single-source — so a conclusion can be audited.

04
Coinages are marked.

New invention words by Douglas are marked (DRS) on first use, so his terms are never mistaken for already-accepted terms.
